About Us

A Trusted Partner for Seafood Sourcing & Export

Bahari Seafood is an Indonesian company engaged in the sourcing, processing, and export of seafood products. We work with selected local fishermen and suppliers to bring squid, octopus, shrimp, crab, lobster, tuna, salmon, and a wide variety of fresh seafood of the finest quality.

Every product goes through careful selection, hygienic handling, and quick freezing to preserve freshness and meet the quality and food-safety standards required by both domestic and international partners.

✓ Sourced directly from partner fishermen & farms
✓ Hygienic handling & quick freezing
✓ Ready for export-scale & wholesale orders
✓ Complete export documentation & permits
