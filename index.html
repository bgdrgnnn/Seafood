About Us

A Trusted Partner for Seafood Sourcing & Export

Bahari Seafood is an Indonesian company engaged in the sourcing, processing, and export of seafood products. We work with selected local fishermen and suppliers to bring squid, octopus, shrimp, lobster, tuna, red snapper, and a wide variety of fresh seafood of the finest quality.

Every product goes through careful selection, hygienic handling, and quick freezing to preserve freshness and meet the quality and food-safety standards required by both domestic and international partners.

✓ Sourced directly from partner fishermen & farms
✓ Hygienic handling & quick freezing
✓ Ready for export-scale & wholesale orders
✓ Complete export documentation & permits

Common Questions from Our Buyers

What's the minimum order (MOQ) for export?

MOQ depends on the product type and destination — from partial container to full container load. Contact our team for a quote tailored to your needs.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept T/T (Telegraphic Transfer) and Letter of Credit (L/C) as agreed with the buyer. Details and terms are discussed during contract negotiation.

Is custom packaging & private label available?

Yes. For export-scale orders, we offer custom packaging and private labeling to your brand's specifications.
